●Private by designfull details ↓

  • No accounts, cookies, analytics or trackers.

00Before you startmost failed flashes are one of these

  1. Browser. Desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera. Firefox 151+ should also work. Safari and iPhone/iPad can't talk to USB devices from a web page. Android Chrome has only just added support and isn't tested here.
  2. Know how your phone will connect. Almost every Meshtastic board has Bluetooth, which is how the phone app talks to it. ESP32 boards (Heltec, LilyGo T-Beam/T-Deck, Station G2 and most others listed in step 03) also have WiFi. nRF52 boards (RAK4631, LilyGo T-Echo, Seeed T1000-E) are Bluetooth only. On ESP32 you pick one: turning WiFi on turns Bluetooth off. If you're not sure, choose Bluetooth in step 01.
  3. A USB data cable, not a charge-only one. Voltage isn't the issue (USB is always 5 V). Cheap charge-only cables simply leave out the data wires, and they look identical. If your computer doesn't see the board, swap cables first. Quick test: a cable that lets your phone show up as a drive on your computer carries data. Most current boards are USB-C; plug straight into the computer, not through a hub or monitor.
  4. USB driver, sometimes. Many ESP32 boards use a CP210x or CH9102 USB chip. Recent macOS and Windows 11 usually handle it on their own; if no port shows up, install the driver from Meshtastic's driver page, then restart the browser.
  5. Antenna on first. Screw the antenna on before plugging the board in. Transmitting without one can damage the radio.
  6. Still not detected? Hold the board's BOOT button while plugging in the cable, then release. Close any other app using the port (Meshtastic app on desktop, serial monitor, Arduino IDE).

02Radio settings

Start here if you're not sure. Your radio keeps the settings Meshtastic ships with; this page only sets the region to United States, plus your name and connection above. That is enough to get on the air and hear anyone nearby who is also on the defaults.

Standard settings come from the firmware itself, so they are right for whichever version gets flashed. For a clean start, use "Erase, flash & configure". "Configure only" leaves a used radio's old preset in place.

Many areas have a local mesh that runs different settings. If you hear nobody, or want better range and fewer lost messages, check whether your area has one.

Outside the United States? Use "Set everything manually" and pick your region.

There is no preset for yet: its radio settings aren't on a public page we could verify.

      ●Privacy & how to check it

      What this page does with your data

      • Nothing leaves your computer except to your radio. Names, Bluetooth PIN, WiFi password and channel keys are sent over the USB cable to the board you plugged in. They aren't uploaded, logged or saved, and they're gone when you close the tab.
      • No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything the page needs is served from this site, including the community presets (the presets/ files, copied in when the site is built).
      • Two outside requests, only when you press a button, both to Meshtastic's own servers:
        • api.meshtastic.org to find the current beta firmware version.
        • release.meshtastic.org to download that firmware, only for "Erase, flash & configure".
        These requests carry no cookies and no referrer. Like any download, Meshtastic's servers see your IP address.
      • Enforced by your browser. The page's Content-Security-Policy tells your browser to block every other connection, so a bug in the page can't send your data anywhere else. The checks below let you confirm the page you got is the published one.
