MeshNewb. get your radios talking

Meshtastic radios send text messages to each other with no cell service and no internet. This page sets them up over a USB cable. Pick what you want to do.

Set up a radio. For a new radio, or one that needs a fresh start. Plug it in, check the name and PIN this page picked for you, press one button.

Join a local mesh. For a radio that is already set up. Pick the mesh near you, plug in, press one button. This tab changes only the radio's mesh settings: modem preset, frequency slot, hop limit and whatever else that group publishes. Its name, Bluetooth or WiFi, PIN and private channels stay as they are.

Family or group channel. Give several radios the same private channel so your household or group can message each other. Create the channel once, then plug in each radio in turn. The channel key is made in this tab and goes to your radios over USB and nowhere else. Do this after every radio is set up.

See how a radio is set. Plug in a radio and press the button. This page reads its settings over USB and shows them here. It changes nothing on the radio. Your radio's settings stay in this tab; the only outside request is to Meshtastic's server, to ask for the current firmware version.

Private. What you type goes to your radio over the USB cable and nowhere else. No accounts, cookies or trackers. The only outside requests ask Meshtastic for the current firmware version and download it. How to check this

00Before you startthree things to have ready

  1. A computer. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a Mac or PC. iPhones, iPads and Safari can't talk to a radio over USB.
  2. A USB cable that carries data. Many cables only charge, and they look identical. If your computer doesn't notice the radio, swap the cable first. Plug straight into the computer, not through a hub or monitor.
  3. The antenna, screwed on. Do this before plugging in. A radio that transmits without its antenna can be damaged.
Radio not showing up?
  • Try another cable. This is the cause most of the time. Quick test: a cable that lets your phone show up on your computer carries data.
  • Close anything else using the radio. The Meshtastic app on this computer, another flasher tab, a serial monitor or Arduino IDE.
  • Install the USB driver. Some radios need one (CP210x or CH9102 chip). Recent macOS and Windows 11 usually have it; if not, get it from Meshtastic's driver page, then restart the browser.
  • Hold the BOOT button. Hold the radio's BOOT button while plugging in the cable, then let go.

Updating an nRF52 board (drag-and-drop)

  1. Download the current Beta file for your board from flasher.meshtastic.org. It is named like .
  2. Plug the board straight into the computer with a data cable (not through a hub). On a Mac laptop, click Allow if asked to allow the accessory.
  3. Put the board in update mode: press the button below. A USB drive appears on your computer (on a T-Echo it is called TECHOBOOT). If nothing happens, double-press the board's Reset button quickly instead.

  5. The drive disappears and the board restarts. A "not ejected properly" warning at this point is normal.

Start here if you're not sure. Your radio keeps the settings Meshtastic ships with; this page only sets the region to United States, plus your name and connection above. That is enough to get on the air and hear anyone nearby who is also on the defaults.

On a used radio, "Just apply my settings" keeps whatever mesh settings its last owner chose. For a clean start, use "Erase and install fresh".

    What does this mean?

    Some people run "gateway" radios that copy mesh traffic to the internet (that link is called MQTT) so it can appear on maps, Discord bridges and stats pages. This box tells those gateways they have your permission to upload your public-channel messages and position.

      ●Privacy & how to check it

      What this page does with your data

      • Nothing leaves your computer except to your radio. Names, Bluetooth PIN, WiFi password and channel keys are sent over the USB cable to the board you plugged in. They aren't uploaded, logged or saved, and they're gone when you close the tab.
      • No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything the page needs is served from this site, including the community presets (the presets/ files, copied in when the site is built).
      • Two outside requests, only when you press a button, both to Meshtastic's own servers:
        • api.meshtastic.org to find the current beta firmware version.
        • release.meshtastic.org to download that firmware, only for "Erase and install fresh".
        These requests carry no cookies and no referrer. Like any download, Meshtastic's servers see your IP address.
      • Enforced by your browser. The page's Content-Security-Policy tells your browser to block every other connection, so a bug in the page can't send your data anywhere else. The checks below let you confirm the page you got is the published one.
