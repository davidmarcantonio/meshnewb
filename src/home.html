MeshNewb. get your radios talking

Meshtastic radios send text messages to each other with no cell service and no internet. This site sets them up over a USB cable. What do you want to do?

Set up a radioA new radio, or one that needs the right settings for your area. Installs the firmware and configures it. Set up a family or group channelGive several radios the same private channel so your household can message each other. Do one radio after another.

Not sure? Start with "Set up a radio". Do the family channel after every radio is set up.

●What you need

  • A desktop browser: Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. Not Safari, not a phone or tablet.
  • A USB data cable. Charge-only cables look identical and are the most common reason a radio isn't found. If one cable doesn't work, try another.
  • The antenna attached before the radio is powered.

●Private by design

  • Names, PINs, passwords and channel keys go from your browser to your radio over the cable, and nowhere else.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. This page runs no scripts at all.
